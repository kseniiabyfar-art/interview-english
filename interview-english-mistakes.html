Task III — Sound More Natural

These aren't grammar mistakes — they're just not how a confident interview answer sounds. Rewrite each one your own way first, then reveal the model answer and compare.

You said: "For short-term / long-term plan..."

Model answer My short-term goal is... / My long-term goal is...
State it as your own goal, not a fragment — "plan for" needs a person and a verb attached to it.

You said: "for now I think I can..."

Model answer I can currently...
"For now" sounds unnatural here — use "currently" instead. "I think I can" also sounds unsure, so state the ability directly.

You said: "I can't adapt to team." — "can't" sounds too negative for an interview.

Model answer It's hard for me to adapt to new changes quickly.
"I can't" sounds like a hard limit. Framing it as something that takes effort ("it's hard for me to...") is honest without sounding like a dealbreaker.

You said: "I very fast do something."

Model answer I do things very quickly. / I pick things up very fast.
The adverb goes after the verb (or verb + object), not before it.

You said: "I prefer if I take 120K a year."

Model answer I'd prefer a salary of 120K a year. / I'd prefer to earn 120K a year.
"Prefer if" isn't a real structure here — use "prefer + noun" or "prefer to + verb."

You said: "some person can quickly change..."

Model answer People who can quickly adapt... / Someone who adapts quickly...
"Some person" sounds vague and slightly odd. For a general experience, use "people"; if you mean one person, use "a person who" or "someone who".

You said: "lovely team"

Model answer a great team / a fantastic team / my favourite team
Not wrong — just too soft/informal for this context. "Lovely" reads as personal warmth rather than professional praise.
